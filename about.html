About LumiMinds

Your trusted partner for comprehensive business solutions

Our Journey

Founded in 2018, LumiMinds began as a vision to bridge the gap between global markets and Zambian businesses and individuals. What started as a vehicle import service has evolved into a comprehensive solutions provider, offering product sourcing and IT services.

Our founders, with backgrounds in international trade, automotive industries, and technology, recognized the need for reliable, transparent, and professional services in Zambia's growing market. They combined their expertise to create a company that not only imports quality products but also provides technological solutions to help businesses thrive in the digital age.

Today, LumiMinds stands as a trusted partner for hundreds of satisfied clients, successfully importing over 500 vehicles and gadgets while delivering numerous IT projects that have transformed businesses across Zambia.
